## Ideas, grouped (106 raw ideas from the readers; full list below)

1. **Auto-forward supplier invoices to PowerOffice** — the most frequent idea (~15 senders: Olmetex, Cervotessile, Somelos, Stork, Afonso, Goldfine, Lisama, Vercel, VWC, Kuroki via Blue.Men, factory invoices via Tex-Challenge, GlobalConnect → ask for EHF). Invoices addressed to the Portuguese company go to HK Consulting instead. → input for the invoice-routing process (step 5/6).
2. **Payments and due dates** — late supplier payments trigger suspensions and reminders (UPS 5× in 2026, Kowa, Quickship, Klasikine, Frameless, rent to Oxer and Kirkegaten 19/21); foreign-currency orders forgotten (Paddlers, Kuroki JPY); payments that silently failed (Cervotessile, US accounts e.g. PrimaLoft); prepayments not matched to proformas (Betina Lou); suppliers asking for proof of payment; Amex payments not reconciled against statements.
3. **Orders typed in by hand** — Eplehuset/PrintCollect order emails (Konsis offers an API), wholesale restocks as free-text size lists (Retro, Standard Agency, Lost in Pablos, Bulifa, Modo's Front Systems CSV), Hobo picks, store-to-customer invoice sales (Pullman), 3PL instructions by email (No More Mondays, ESP Oslo); a B2B portal for re-orders and order status has been promised.
4. **Production and shipping** — price-review replies in mixed formats; production-plan confirmations by email instead of in the portal; POs cannot be revised; generated POs lack supplier article codes; no tracker for inbound Japan shipments (Rangel); customs document packs for vintage imports and EU origin; unrecoverable Portuguese import VAT on non-EU materials; factories want forecasts early; label details (pre-punched QR labels).
5. **System monitoring** — Sitoo→Cin7 sales/transfer sync failures found by hand (eMonkey); Shopify fraud-flagged orders flowing to Cin7; no price sync Origo/Loom → Sitoo/Shopify; Loom lacks FedEx; Pio stock reservation for pre-orders; Threadflow improvement list.
6. **Mail routing** — review-mail replies go to the CFO instead of help@; webshop support lacks Shopify rights (gift cards, VAT receipts); daily courier bookings land in the CFO's inbox; bought-in brands send invoices via colleagues instead of invoice@.
7. **Checklists and calendar** — contractor and staff offboarding (access, mailing lists); 3PL client onboarding; year-end balance clean-up; intercompany gift-card postings; bank deadlines (annual accounts, Eksfin renewal, credit-line expiry, KYC); Galleriet annual turnover confirmation; apply for trade-fair support before invoices are dated; staff travel booking routine.

- (suppliers/albano-morgado.md) A payment to Albano Morgado (2026-06) was stuck on a SWIFT/BIC error in the accounting software for about a week before the supplier chased it; check supplier bank details when registering foreign suppliers, and check rejected/stuck payments.

- (operations@livid.no (Livid)) Replies to the seasonal price review (sent from operations@livid.no to ~20 suppliers) come back in mixed formats (CSV, Excel, PDF, email text) and must be consolidated by hand into the price list.
