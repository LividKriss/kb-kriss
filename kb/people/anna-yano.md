# Who

# Relationship
Kowa supplies fabrics (stock-service articles) for Livid's samples and bulk. The goods ship straight to Livid's manufacturers (Siluete, Dia, Stork, Lisama). Annie confirms POs, sends order confirmations (OCs), AWBs and courier invoices, and issues the debit invoices ("Nego Docs #A…"). She also chases payments. Contact with Kowa goes back to 2025-03-26 in the index, and threads come roughly monthly, more often around shipments and due dates.
