# Relationship
Day-to-day contact between the Norwegian retailers and Livid. He forwards restock (supplering) orders, claims (reklamasjon), sponsor-jeans requests, order confirmation (OC) and masterdata requests, and he chases overdue retailer payments when Kriss asks. Very frequent contact: 602 threads in 3 years, Kriss replied in 59 in the last 12 months.
