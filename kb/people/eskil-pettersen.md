# Who
Accountant at EP Regnskap AS (signs "EP"). He handles year-end work for Livid Jeans AS and Livid Retail AS, and also for Dankertsen Holding AS: annual accounts, tax returns, shareholder register and signing rounds. Livid does its own bookkeeping in PowerOffice.

# Relationship
They have been in contact since at least 2023-11-08. The work peaks every June–July with the annual accounts: Eskil drafts the accounts, Kriss answers his questions on the balance and passes the drafts to the auditor, and Eskil sends the accounts for signing and files them. Shareholder-register questions come up in December/January. The rest of the year Kriss asks him ad hoc how to post things. Eskil usually opens each season by asking whether EP should help again.

# How Kriss communicates with them
Norwegian. Opens with "Hei Eskil," and often "Håper alt er bra." Signs "Kristoffer" or doesn't sign at all. Messages are short, and replies can be a single line ("Ingen endringer", "Flott!", "Yes ..."). When Eskil sends a list of questions, Kriss answers inline, point by point. He copies Jens Olav Dankertsen on Dankertsen Holding matters. Eskil writes tersely ("Hei." … "Mvh EP"), so short answers are fine.
