# Summary
From 47 messages, 2025-10 to 2026-10 (3 truncated). He writes in two modes. Rent reminders, guarantee claims and revenue reporting get short, courteous replies: he apologises fast, gives a brief reason and promises a fix. Lease extensions, payment-deferral requests and the rent renegotiation get formal, well-argued letters. These use figures and market reasoning, ask clearly, stay firm on Livid's position, and always end by inviting the landlord's response.

# Language
- Norwegian in 47/47, including with property managers at large real-estate firms.
- Bokmål with occasional dialect or spoken forms ("såg", "komt", "Hm ja"). Keep drafts in standard bokmål.

# Formality and length
- **Admin replies (about two thirds):** 1–4 lines, often a single sentence ("Ja 5 år stemmer", "Dere kan godt komme hit", "Det er i orden, send over til signering"). He confirms, attaches proof ("Vedlagt betalingsbekreftelse, for ordens skyld") or says when it will be fixed.
- **Formal letters (about 12):** 4–7 paragraphs, 200–400 words. Pattern: purpose sentence ("Jeg kontakter dere i dag med en forespørsel om…") → context with numbers (growth %, revenue, rent per year, square metres) → the concrete ask with an alternative ("Alternativt, dersom lettere for dere, …") → reassurance ("Jeg vil understreke at dette er et kortsiktig behov…") → invitation to reply.
- **Negotiation:** firm and specific. He names a ceiling ("et leienivå over <amount>/året"), states the consequence calmly ("styrer vi heller mot en kontrollert avvikling i <Place>"), acknowledges the other side ("Vi forstår selvsagt at dere har deres egne vurderinger…") and offers a middle path. No bluffing language, no emotion.
- **Urgency:** when a bank guarantee claim is involved he pushes clearly and explains why ("banken har hast med å få orden på dette", "Frist på betaling for banken er i dag"), and escalates politely ("…må vi nesten få noen andre i <Company> til å se på dette").
- **Proactive fixes:** he asks to be put on reminders ("Flott også om min epost kan settes opp på eventuelle purringer, så unngår vi at slikt skjer igjen!").
- No bullet points or headings (0 in 47). Even letters with several figures are written as prose.
