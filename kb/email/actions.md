# Applies to
Every thread the [daily run](/email/daily-run.md) labels.

# Rules
- **AI/Urgent, AI/Reply:** stay in the inbox. Draft a reply as a Gmail draft in the thread (thread + Person note + Voice Profile + relevant livid-kb docs), in the thread's language, ending with Kriss's sign-off. No disclaimer in the draft; it is added only when the assistant sends it ([signature](/email/signature.md), step 5b). No draft when the answer needs a decision only Kriss can make — then the brief says what must be decided.
- **AI/Waiting:** when Kriss's question has had no answer for **3 working days** (**2** for production and payment threads), draft a short polite nudge for his approval and list it in the brief.
- **AI/FYI:** archive; one line in the brief.
- **AI/Noise:** archive; counted in the brief, listed only on request.
- **Invoices and customs documents:** per [invoice routing](kb://livid/finance/bookkeeping/processes/invoice-routing.md) — vouchers in an approval batch, customs documents filed.
- **Sending, forwarding, replying:** only what Kriss approves in the run's session ("send 1, 3", "forward 1–7"), until `email/send-policy.md` (step 5b) is stable. After sending: archive the thread, move the label (Waiting if a question was asked).
- **Never:** delete mail, mark spam, unsubscribe, accept invitations, add recipients, or send attachments with financial or personal data without explicit approval of that item.
